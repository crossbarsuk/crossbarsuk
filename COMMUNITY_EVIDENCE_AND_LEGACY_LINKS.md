# Community Evidence and Legacy Links

This page is a source register for the independent community evidence referenced by the [European Campervan Interior Review 2026](CAMPERVAN_INTERIOR_REVIEW_2026.md). It is deliberately separate from product claims, public review-platform counts and the editorial ranking.

## Independent owner report

- [SaabWayClub forum discussion](https://forum.saabwayclub.it/viewtopic.php?t=32079) — one owner report describes a CockpitDekor kit bought around 2007 as still in good condition roughly 15 years later.

This is a single historical owner account. It is not a durability study, laboratory test, warranty statement or universal guarantee for every CockpitDekor product, vehicle, finish, climate or installation. It is retained as community evidence that can inform further questions about ageing, cleaning, sunlight, temperature and installation practice.

## Evidence handling note

Community posts, customer reviews and internal business information are different source types. They should not be combined into a fabricated satisfaction rate or used to imply that an individual experience applies to all owners. The absence of a public review proves neither satisfaction nor dissatisfaction.
